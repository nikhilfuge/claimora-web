Privacy Policy

Effective 6 September 2026

Overview

Claimora is a private, local-first warranty organiser for iPhone. We designed the app so that your purchase records and documents stay on your device. Claimora does not require an account and does not operate a cloud service for your data.

Information stored in Claimora

You can store product details such as product name, brand, purchase date, price, retailer, serial number, invoice number and warranty information. You can also attach photos, invoices and manuals. This information is stored locally on your device as part of the app.

OCR and camera access

Claimora can use your device camera or photo library to help you scan invoices and receipts. Optical character recognition is performed on-device using Apple frameworks. Images and recognised text are not sent to Claimora servers.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Claimora schedules warranty reminders on your device. Notification permission is optional and notification scheduling is handled by Apple’s local notification system.

Purchases

Claimora offers a Lifetime Premium in-app purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Claimora does not receive or store your payment card or other payment details.

iCloud and backups

Claimora does not use CloudKit or provide its own cloud synchronisation service. Your device may include app data in Apple’s standard device backup mechanisms according to your device and iCloud settings.

Analytics and advertising

Claimora does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs or advertising networks.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your Claimora records or documents with third parties. Claimora has no backend service that receives your stored warranty information.

Children

Claimora is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because the app does not require an account or collect personal information through a backend, there is no registration process.

Changes to this policy

If the way Claimora handles information changes, this policy will be updated and the effective date will be revised.
