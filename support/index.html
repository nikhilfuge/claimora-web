Claimora Support

Need help with Claimora? We’re happy to help.

Contact us

Email neelstudios2019@gmail.com with a description of the issue. If possible, include your iPhone model, iOS version and the Claimora version shown in Settings.

Before contacting support

  • Make sure you are using the latest version of Claimora available from the App Store.
  • If an invoice scan produced incorrect details, you can review and edit the recognised information before saving.
  • If reminders are not appearing, check that notifications are enabled for Claimora in iPhone Settings.

Privacy

Claimora is designed to keep your warranty information on your device. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
